# Home product screenshots

Captured from the public Navio Explore Plans and Guides pages on 2026-11-14.
These depict the actual application and published content, not mock interfaces
or fabricated usage metrics. Requests using POST were blocked during capture.

The surrounding navigation and lower engagement row are cropped out. Assets
are compressed to WebP (1152 × 850); no interface or content was composited.
They are static product illustrations, not a live feed. Refresh captures when
Explore changes. The Home caption explicitly identifies them as screenshots.

Destination photos retain their original licenses. See the adjacent
`../plan-covers/README.md`, catalog attribution metadata, the source Explore
pages and Home's photography credits. Existing bundled destination photos are
reused for the hero and closing section; no new generated photography is used.
